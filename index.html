The problem · UK-wide

Commercial rooftops are the UK's
most wasted solar asset.

Installers

  • Spare installation capacity sits idle
  • Days lost manually hunting for leads
  • Cold calls and bought lists convert poorly

Businesses

  • Don't know they're strong solar candidates
  • Underestimate savings; lack in-house expertise
  • Most are never contacted at all
  • Protect against high electricity costs

The grid

  • Reduces peak national demand
  • Eases transmission congestion
  • Cheap distributed generation goes unbuilt

This is a failure of discovery — not of technology, economics, or demand.

Opportunities & threats

Data quality→Confidence score on every estimate
Prediction uncertainty→Explainable rankings, not black boxes
Planning constraints→Flagged per site · human review
User trust→Installer-in-the-loop · continuous model improvement
No public record of commercial buildings with solar→Solar-presence agent infers it; installer feedback verifies
No calibration data yet→Pilot feedback loop builds it from real installs
Opportunity→The method extends to the whole world
